About MoneyClever

MoneyClever is a free, open-source financial literacy platform. We believe that honest, data-driven financial education should be accessible to everyone — from someone who just lost their job and is looking for solutions, to experienced investors wanting to expand their portfolio.

Every strategy on this platform shows its risks front and center. We don't sell anything, we don't take commissions, and we don't promote specific financial products. Our goal is simple: help you make informed decisions.

This is not financial advice. Always consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.

Our Values

  • Honesty First — Risk is always front and center. No hype.
  • Data-Driven — Evidence ratings show how proven each strategy is.
  • Accessible — Free, no ads, no tracking, open to all.
  • No BS — We tell you what works, what doesn't, and what can go wrong.
