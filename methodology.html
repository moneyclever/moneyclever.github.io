Data Sources & Methodology

All strategy data on MoneyClever is compiled from publicly available sources including academic research, financial regulatory filings, industry reports, and established financial education resources.

Evidence Rating Scale

  • 5 — Extensive Proof: Decades of data, multiple meta-analyses, widely accepted
  • 4 — Strong Evidence: Significant research backing, proven track record
  • 3 — Well Documented: Published studies and real-world data available
  • 2 — Some Data: Limited studies or primarily observational data
  • 1 — Anecdotal: Primarily based on individual experiences

Risk Rating Scale

  • 1 — Very Low: Capital preservation, minimal chance of loss
  • 2 — Low: Small potential for loss, generally stable
  • 3 — Moderate: Notable risk of loss, requires knowledge
  • 4 — High: Significant risk of substantial loss
  • 5 — Very High: Extreme risk, potential for total loss of capital
